First Trace — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026 · Русская версия

First Trace is a logic puzzle game for a general audience of teenagers and adults (13+). This policy explains what happens to your data when you play it.

What the game collects

Nothing. The current version of First Trace does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services, and it does not need an internet connection.

What is stored on your device

Your level progress, scores, in-game crystals, unlocked items and settings (language, sound, controls, chosen hero and board) are saved only in the game's own storage on your device. This data never leaves the device. Deleting the game deletes it.

Children

First Trace is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because the game collects no personal information at all.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising or any other service that processes data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, listing each service and what it receives. The effective date above will change.
